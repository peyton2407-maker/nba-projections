Product Brief

Interactive own-model board for NBA game projections (moneyline, spread, total) and player props, benchmarked against DraftKings.

  • Markets: Full-game ML / spread / total + player PTS, REB, AST, 3PM, PRA, STL, BLK.
  • Model: Projected minutes × recent per-minute rates, adjusted for pace, home/away, rest, and basic vacancy. Residual layer (fading α) activates after games are played.
  • Odds: DraftKings primary. Edges shown as model minus DK line.
  • Differentiator: Transparent numbers, residual tracking, clean board. No opaque confidence scores.
